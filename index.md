# SpareDeck

SpareDeck turns a spare phone or tablet into a control deck for your own Mac or Windows PC: app launchers, shortcuts, volume, media, timer and more, over your local network.

- [Privacy policy](privacy) · [Informativa sulla privacy](privacy-it)
- Support: **sparedeckpolicy@protonmail.com**

## Support

**My computer doesn't show up in the app.** The phone and the computer must be on the same Wi-Fi, and the SpareDeck server must be running on the computer. You can also tap **Scan QR** and scan the code from the computer's menu bar / tray menu.

**The app asks to approve the device.** Every new phone or tablet must be allowed once on the computer (an Allow / Deny dialog appears there).

**Shortcut or text keys do nothing on a Mac.** Give SpareDeck the Accessibility permission in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.

**I want to remove a device.** On the computer, revoke it from the menu bar / tray menu. On the phone, tap **Remove** next to the computer.

For anything else, write to **sparedeckpolicy@protonmail.com**.
